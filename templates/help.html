Everything you need to know about using Business Finder

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for businesses?
Enter what you're looking for (like "coffee" or "pizza") in the search box, specify your location, and optionally filter by category. Click "Search Businesses" to see results from local businesses in your area.
How do I filter and sort results?
Use the "Category" dropdown to filter by business type (food, retail, services, etc.). Use the "Sort By" dropdown to organize results by relevance, highest rated, or most reviews.
What does the star icon mean?
The star icon lets you bookmark your favorite businesses. Click the empty star (☆) to save a business. Click the filled star (⭐) to remove it from your bookmarks. Your bookmarks are saved automatically.
How do I leave a review?
Click on any business to view its details page. Scroll down to the review form, enter your name, select a star rating (1-5), write your review (optional), and complete the "I'm not a robot" verification. Click "Submit Review" to save your feedback.
Why do I need to verify I'm not a robot?
We use Google reCAPTCHA to prevent automated bots from submitting fake reviews. This keeps our reviews authentic and trustworthy. Simply check the "I'm not a robot" box before submitting your review.
What are special deals and coupons?
Some businesses offer exclusive deals and discounts. Look for the "Special Deals" badge on business cards in search results, or check the business detail page for current offers with discount percentages and expiration dates.
How do I access the admin panel?
Business owners and administrators can manage deals by visiting the admin panel at /admin. From there, you can add new deals, view existing offers, and delete expired promotions.
